## 2026-11-09 — Read redirect hops from history title twins before writing a starter rule

Change: When history certification finds an unmatched route, the grader should flag "title twins": an unmatched URL whose visit count and title set equal those of a matched URL on a sibling host or route (Chrome copies the final page's title onto every URL in a server-redirect chain). Print each unmatched URL's twin in the grading output, and add one line to the spec's "Research method": a twin is a redirect hop, so close the gap with the reason instead of adding a rule. Why: on 2026-11-09 three of the six coverage-gap beads were redirect hops, not missing rules: `<company>.box.com/s/<name>` (twin of `<company>.app.box.com/s/<name>`), `ldr.php?RCID=` (twin of the recordingservice player) and `j.php?MTID=` (twin of the join launcher). Each took a manual cross-reference to see, and a rule on any of them would have recorded a second artifact for a page Brauser already records. Where: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-starter-families-design.md` "Research method"; the history-certification grading script.
